Redeemed Mobile Mechanic — Draft Service Agreement

Service location: Customer-provided location within the agreed service area.

  1. Authorization: The customer authorizes inspection and only the repair work and charges separately approved by the customer.
  2. Estimates: Diagnostics, labor, parts, travel charges and applicable fees will be disclosed for customer approval before chargeable work begins.
  3. Additional work: Any additional work or cost requires the customer's approval.
  4. Access and safety: The customer agrees to provide safe, lawful access to the vehicle. Work may be postponed if conditions are unsafe.
  5. Deposits: A deposit may be required on a case-by-case basis depending on the type and scope of work, parts or materials needed, and estimated costs. Any required deposit amount, payment deadline, and whether it is refundable will be explained to the customer and agreed upon before work begins. Deposits are not required for every service.
  6. Payment: Payment method and timing will be agreed before service. No automatic payment is collected by this page.
  7. Limitations: Repairs depend on parts availability, vehicle condition and safe working conditions. Any warranty terms must be provided separately in writing.
